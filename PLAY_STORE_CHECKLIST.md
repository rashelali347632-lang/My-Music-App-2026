# My Music — Play Store Release Checklist

## App identity
- App name: My Music
- Version: 1.0.0
- Package ID: com.example.onlinemusic

## Before upload
1. Replace all `YOUR-DOMAIN.com` MP3 URLs with URLs for music you own or are licensed to stream.
2. Create a unique 512x512 PNG app icon and add it to the Android project.
3. Create a public privacy-policy web page and enter its URL in Play Console.
4. Prepare a feature graphic and phone screenshots for the store listing.
5. Complete Data Safety, Content Rating, target audience, and app-content declarations.
6. Build a **signed Android App Bundle (.aab)** from Android Studio.
7. Upload the AAB to a testing track first.

## Important
Do not upload copyrighted music or artwork without permission. Do not use Spotify branding, logos, or copyrighted assets as if this app were an official Spotify product.

## Suggested store listing
Short description:
"Stream your favorite online music with search, favorites and a simple dark player."

Full description:
"My Music is an online music player for streaming audio from authorized sources. Search songs, play and pause tracks, save favorites, and control playback from the mini player. Music and artwork are supplied by the app owner or licensed providers."
